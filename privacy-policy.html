Privacy Policy

How Your Life Guard collects, holds, uses and discloses your personal information.

Last updated 21 September 2026

About this policy

This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with and how you can see or correct it.

"We" and "us" means Your Life Guard Pty Ltd (ABN 93 647 912 347). We are an authorised representative of Life Experts Pty Ltd (ABN 35 647 911 671, AFSL 533827).

We follow the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles. Personal information means any information about you that identifies you, or could reasonably identify you.

Why we collect your information

We collect personal information to:

  • give you quotes and compare life insurance options
  • help you apply for cover and place it with an insurer
  • look after your policy after it starts
  • contact you later to see if your cover still suits you
  • tell you about other products and services we offer
  • meet our legal duties as a financial services business
  • handle any complaint you make

If you don't give us the information we ask for, we may not be able to give you a quote or help you apply.

What we collect

To give you a quote we usually ask for your:

  • name, date of birth and gender
  • address, phone number and email
  • occupation and income
  • details of any insurance you already hold

To apply for cover, insurers also need health and lifestyle information. This includes your medical history, smoking status, hobbies and sometimes information from your doctor. This is sensitive information under the Privacy Act. We only collect it with your consent and only to place or manage your cover.

We may also ask for details of other people, such as a partner you want covered or the person you want a claim paid to. Please only give us their details if they know you are doing so and agree.

How we collect it

Most of the time you give it to us directly, by phone, email, text message or our website forms.

Sometimes your enquiry starts on another company's website. We buy enquiries from lead generation businesses. If you fill in a form on one of their sites asking to compare life insurance, they send your details to us and we contact you.

We may also collect information from:

  • insurers, about a policy you hold or are applying for
  • the insurer, which may send us a copy of the medical screening your doctor or nurse did for your application, with your signed permission
  • Life Experts Pty Ltd, our licensee
  • public sources such as social media, where the law allows

We record all calls for compliance and training, and to keep a record of the conversation if it is ever needed.

Who we share it with

We share your information with:

  • insurers you ask us to get quotes from, or that you apply to
  • Life Experts Pty Ltd, which holds the financial services licence we operate under and checks our work
  • medical services that arrange health checks for your application
  • companies that provide our systems, such as our customer database, phone system and email
  • our staff and contractors, including offshore teams (see below)
  • regulators, courts and dispute bodies when the law requires it

Insurers and other companies we share your information with have their own privacy policies.

We do not sell your personal information.

Some of our team is overseas

We use staff and service providers in the Philippines and India. They can see your information to do their work, under contracts that require them to protect it. All our records are stored in our customer database on servers in Australia.

We will contact you again

Life changes, and so do insurance prices. We keep your details after your enquiry or policy so we can contact you later. We may call, email or text you to:

  • ask whether your cover still suits you
  • let you know if a better-priced policy is available
  • tell you about other products and services we offer, now or in the future

You can opt out at any time. Every marketing email and text has an unsubscribe link, or you can call us on 1300 718 859. If you opt out, we will still contact you about a policy you hold with us when we need to.

How long we keep it

We keep records of the advice we give and the policies we arrange for at least seven years, as financial services law requires, and often longer. When we no longer need information we delete it, or strip out anything that identifies you.

How we protect it

We keep your information in secure systems with access limited to staff who need it. Staff are trained in privacy and bound by confidentiality. If your information is lost, stolen or accessed without permission, and that is likely to cause you serious harm, the law requires us to tell you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner. We will do that as soon as we can, and tell you what you can do to protect yourself.

Our website

Our website uses cookies and analytics tools from Google and Bing to see how people use the site. This records your IP address, the pages you visit and the site you came from. It does not identify you by name. You can block cookies in your browser, but some parts of the site may not work.

If you share a page from our site on social media, that platform's privacy policy applies to what it collects.

You can see and correct your information

You can ask for a copy of the information we hold about you. We will respond within 30 days. There is no charge to ask. We may charge a small fee to cover copying if the request is large.

If anything we hold is wrong or out of date, tell us and we will fix it.

In a few cases the law lets us refuse access, for example if it would affect someone else's privacy. If we refuse, we will tell you why in writing.

How to complain

If you think we have mishandled your information, contact us:

Email: enquiries@yourlifeguard.com.au

Telephone: 1300 718 859

Put "Complaint" in the subject line and tell us what happened and the outcome you want. We will acknowledge your complaint within one business day and respond within 30 calendar days. If we need more time, we will tell you why.

If you are not happy with our response, or 30 days have passed, you can take it to:

  • Australian Financial Complaints Authority (AFCA), 1800 931 678, www.afca.org.au. Our licensee Life Experts Pty Ltd is AFCA member 83212.
  • Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC), 1300 363 992, www.oaic.gov.au

Changes to this policy

We update this policy when our practices change.
